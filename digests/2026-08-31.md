**MaxPO closes a theoretical gap in the GRPO advantage-estimation thread.** The vault's GRPO papers (GRPO-base, Hints/Critics/Teachers, OPDVR, AToD) all use or modify GRPO's advantage estimator without questioning its centering property. MaxPO proves that GRPO's estimator is policy-gradient unbiased but non-centered (non-zero batch sum), and that L2O centering reduces gradient variance with no loss of unbiasedness. This is an immediately actionable finding: every vault paper using GRPO-style group advantage could benefit from the L2O swap. The Hints/Critics/Teachers HL-Gauss vs. MSE critic finding (Aug 30) + MaxPO L2O centering finding (today) form a two-paper action list for improving GRPO training: better critic loss + better baseline.

**TeamTR provides the theoretical foundation that SAT assumed.** SAT (Aug 29) proved plug-and-play invariance and monotonic improvement for block-coordinate multi-LLM training but did not formalize why naive sequential fine-tuning fails. TeamTR formalizes exactly this: compounding occupancy shift in cached rollouts incurs O(N²) evaluation penalty. The penalty theorem explains SAT's empirical motivation from first principles. Together, SAT + TeamTR constitute a two-paper theoretical foundation for multi-LLM team RL training, analogous to how ATOD + OPDVR (Aug 29-30) form complementary solutions to OPD/RL integration.

**CEB introduces a hindsight pseudo-label pattern at inference time, not training time.** The five credit-assignment vault papers (SPA-RL, MileGPO, DataPRM/ToolPRM, IAPO, EFCA — Aug 30) all apply hindsight reasoning at training time to generate better gradient signals. CEB applies the identical structural idea — "what does the outcome tell us about each intermediate step?" — at inference time to generate calibrated confidence estimates. This is the first vault paper to deploy the hindsight pattern for deployment-time confidence rather than training-time credit, suggesting a unified pattern: retrospective outcome labeling of intermediate steps is useful at every stage of the RL pipeline.

**GBT (Traversal-as-Policy) adds a fourth position to the distillation design space axis.** Prior axis (from Aug 30 SPT analysis): (a) pre-training absorption (SPT), (b) RL-time library evolution (SkillForge/Skill-R1/Skill1), (c) training-time trajectory signal (PACT). GBT adds: (d) runtime policy replacement — the distilled knowledge lives as an external executable tree, not in model parameters, and is invoked at generation time whenever a task is in coverage. This is the only vault approach where the distilled policy is inspectable and auditable without probing the model weights.
