**AgentBrew operationalizes offline credit assignment for real-world tool-use without verifiers.** The combination of retrospective task inference (outcome→instruction reconstruction) + PMI-based per-action credit is the first offline training signal for tool-use agents that requires neither pre-specified tasks nor a simulator. This places AgentBrew in the same design space as DMPO (which also avoids online rollouts via occupancy-measure constraint) and Auto-Dreamer (which avoids online rollouts via offline GRPO memory) but targets real-world tool-use APIs rather than simulated environments. PMI credit is structurally distinct from TRIAL's signed log-probability gap and T-STAR's cross-trajectory cognitive tree: it is information-theoretic rather than distribution-comparative or trajectory-structural.

**SVRL closes a sub-gap in the SFT-free VLM RL thread.** Previous VLM RL papers (SPIRAL, Odysseus, PROPA) either required SFT warm-start or focused on single-round visual QA. SVRL demonstrates GRPO-only training of a multimodal agent with iterative tool use (web search) and a compound reward (correctness + search efficiency + query diversity) — no SFT cold-start. The search-aware penalty is a novel reward component not present in any prior agentic RL paper: it directly penalizes tool-call overhead within the RL objective rather than treating efficiency as a post-hoc metric. This extends the reward engineering thread from execution-validity (ExecCritic) and probe-consensus (ERPO) to tool-efficiency.

**SRPO is the formal GRPO extension for multi-agent LLM pipelines.** GRPO optimizes individual responses in isolation; SRPO identifies the active set (outputs jointly consumed by one transition) as the correct optimization unit for multi-agent settings. The cardinality normalization is mathematically necessary because a set of N outputs should have the same gradient magnitude as a single output, not N times larger. This is the multi-agent analog of GRPO's group normalization for single-agent advantage estimation. SRPO + SAPO (shared autoregressive backbone) + SAO (single-rollout-per-prompt) + SRPO (setwise joint action) represent three orthogonal efficiency/correctness improvements to GRPO that have not been composed.

**Experience Funnel and NeoHorse-1 together define model-harness co-evolution as a design space.** Experience Funnel formalizes the fast-state/slow-policy alternation: explicit harness is the fast channel, parametric policy is the slow channel, and transition-aware distillation is the conversion mechanism. NeoHorse-1 makes the harness an active data collection signal: routing choices are recorded, fed back through evaluation, and shape the next training mixture. Neither paper jointly optimizes harness and policy weights under an explicit compatibility objective (the model-harness gap identified from Co-Evolving Harnesses), but both contribute necessary components toward it.
