**The skill-evolution thread now has a four-paper taxonomy.** The vault's prior skill-reuse coverage (SkillGate Aug 20, SPyCE Aug 23) focused on credit for skill selection and co-evolution of skill libraries with the policy. Today adds three papers that collectively span the architectural design space for skill evolution: (1) **SkillForge** — continuous verification-and-pruning loop within append-based SkillRL; solves the stale-skill problem. (2) **Skill-R1** — separate lightweight skill generator trained while task LLM is frozen; bi-level GRPO for recurrent revision; covers the closed-source/frozen-LLM corner. (3) **Skill1** — single policy, single reward, signal decomposed by frequency (low → selection, high → distillation); the most parsimonious architecture. (4) **SKILL0** (outside cap) — skills internalized into parameters via progressive curriculum withdrawal; zero-shot at inference time. These four designs vary on two orthogonal axes: *architectural separation* (unified vs. separate generator) and *temporal structure* (single-round vs. recurrent vs. curriculum-withdrawal). Neither Skill-R1 nor Skill1 appears to cite the other, suggesting independent development of the recurrent credit decomposition idea.

**MAPO identifies the reasoning-action discrepancy as a new failure mode for VLM RL.** The prior VLM RL thread (SPyCE Aug 23 — skill co-evolution, GEAR Aug 23 — evidence-grounding reward, EvolveVLA Aug 20 — on-the-fly tool use) all assumed that if the textual reasoning is correct and the task succeeds, the training signal is clean. MAPO shows this assumption fails when visual tools are involved: a model can produce fluent textual reasoning while invoking visual tools with imprecise parameters, and outcome rewards will credit the trajectory regardless. The description-alignment reward is the VLM-agent analogue of GEAR's evidence-grounding reward — both require the model to produce a verifiable text representation of the environmental evidence it used. MAPO's theoretical result (variance reduction in the advantage estimate) is also the strongest formal justification in the vault for any reward-shaping approach since PlanPO's length-conditioned advantage analysis.

**DataPRM extends the PRM/credit thread to agentic data analysis with two new taxonomic concepts.** "Silent errors" (incorrect intermediate results that don't raise exceptions) and "exploratory action penalization" (penalizing necessary trial-and-error) are the data-analysis domain's analogues of the two failure modes in "Credit Without Ground Truth" (Aug 21): step correctness tracking vs. causal contribution. The environment-aware verifier that actively probes intermediate states is the first vault paper to implement active verification (as opposed to passive trajectory annotation), structurally analogous to but more general than Verifiable Counterfactual Supervision's template injection.
