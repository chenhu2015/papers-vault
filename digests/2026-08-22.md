**Cross-View Correspondence challenges every vault paper that assigns turn-level credit.** RTPO (Aug 20), PlanPO (Aug 21), MileGPO (Aug 21), SkillGate (Aug 20), and GUPO (Aug 19) all assign credit at the turn or step level. This paper shows that the correspondence used to localise credit temporally is not uniquely defined — two optimal correspondences can disagree on 55.9% of turns. The implication is not that these papers are wrong, but that their credit assignment procedures need to be declared and validated using the two-sided framework. Papers that use frozen replications (as this paper recommends) are more trustworthy than those that use online correspondence.
