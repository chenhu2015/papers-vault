**Gated-BEPO introduces empirical Bellman backup as a new step-level credit mechanism.** The vault's step-level credit taxonomy has focused on OPD/distillation-based approaches (Guided-OPD: state selection, FutureBridge-OPD: future trajectory validation, RP-OPSD: reasoning-pivot token detection). Gated-BEPO introduces a fundamentally different source: TD-style Bellman backup from empirical rollout graphs, without a separate critic network or teacher. The empirical graph (constructed from matched-state rollout groups) provides value estimates from the current policy's action distribution; GAE accumulates TD residuals along trajectories. The confidence gate (Bellman credit only when multiple successors are observed) is a novel adaptive fusion mechanism that acknowledges the empirical grounding problem: step credit is only meaningful when sufficient rollout diversity exists at that state. This is orthogonal to IBPO's implicit multi-trajectory credit (which uses trajectory divergence as step signal) and TACO's counterfactual credit (which counterfactually removes tool calls).

**VAD bridges the CSCR counterfactual thread with the multimodal OPD shortcut thread.** Two active vault threads converge in VAD: (A) CSCR's counterfactual robustness — test whether signal direction changes under an alternative condition; (B) the multimodal OPD shortcut — teacher corrections are source-mixed, containing visual signal + linguistic priors + teacher-specific effects. VAD applies CSCR-style counterfactual logic at the OPD supervision level: "does the teacher direction change when visual evidence is removed?" The projection of the teacher correction onto the visual evidence direction yields an intervention-aligned component that is (by construction) visually grounded. The teacher becomes a weak regularizer for the residual. This is the first paper in the vault that uses counterfactual evidence attribution rather than divergence thresholding or confidence weighting to filter OPD corrections. Key distinction from ViGOS: ViGOS decouples perception and reasoning stages with separate teachers; VAD keeps a single teacher but decomposes corrections within each prefix.

**AdaKP provides the online curriculum counterpart to AES-HDC's environment quality selection.** The vault's curriculum literature has approached quality-vs-quantity at the environment level (AES-HDC: ability-aware environment selection beats raw count) and the training-data level (Internalizing Curriculum Judgment). AdaKP operates at a new level: the knowledge-hint injection level in prompt-based RL training. The entropy-reduction proxy (how much does this hint compress the next-token distribution?) is a direct signal of hint informativeness that avoids expensive rollout estimation. The retirement-revival mechanism parallels GRPO's all-fail filtering thread (Gap #16): KPs that consistently fail to reduce entropy are retired, preserving training capacity for informative hints.

**OPPO extends Gap #7 from visual to omni-modal faithfulness.** The vault's Gap #7 thread has been partially closed: IAPO (visual faithfulness as reward in VLM RL), ViGOS (decoupled perception-reasoning OPD to prevent visual shortcuts), and now OPPO (cue-decomposed reward + masked-input KL for audio+visual emotion reasoning). OPPO's most transferable contribution is the Omni-Perception Loss: by applying KL penalties on modality-specific tokens under masked input, it directly supervises the model to not hallucinate from one modality into another. This is a general mechanism applicable beyond emotion reasoning — any multimodal RL setting where cross-modal hallucination is a problem. The structural parallel with VAD: VAD attributes visual evidence at the OPD correction level; OPPO enforces visual faithfulness at the RL loss level.
