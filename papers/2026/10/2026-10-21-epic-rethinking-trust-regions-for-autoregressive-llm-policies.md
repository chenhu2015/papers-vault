# EPIC: Rethinking Trust Regions for Autoregressive LLM Policies via Exact-KL Prefix Importance-Ratio Clipping

## Summary

EPIC identifies a critical flaw in token-level PPO clipping for autoregressive LLMs: standard per-token importance ratios miss the cumulative prefix drift because the prefix itself is generated by the policy, making the token-level measure incomplete. By reformulating LLM generation as a finite-horizon MDP and deriving exact prefix-level importance weights (cumulative likelihood ratio over the generated prefix), EPIC constructs a prefix-level trust region using exact reverse-KL geometry (r−1−log r), yielding principled sequence-level clipping that outperforms standard and sequence-level baselines on mathematical reasoning in off-policy settings.

## Key Contributions

- Formal MDP reformulation of autoregressive generation + performance-difference identity under behavior-policy sampling
- Exact importance weight for each stepwise policy-improvement term = cumulative prefix likelihood ratio
- Prefix-level trust region using exact reverse-KL coordinate r−1−log r (behavior-policy expectation = prefix-distribution KL)
- EPIC algorithm: PPO-style sequence clipping without local quadratic approximation or expert-routing replay
- Strong results on off-policy RL with sparse MoE LLM across mathematical reasoning benchmarks

## Relevance

Directly extends the LGPO finding (IS ratio is unnecessary for stability in on-policy GRPO) by showing what the EXACT prefix-level IS ratio should be — LGPO showed token-level IS correction is inert in on-policy regimes; EPIC shows the full formulation needed in off-policy regimes where prefix drift is non-trivial. Together they bracket the importance-sampling landscape in LLM RL.
