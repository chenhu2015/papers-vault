## Open gaps (updated)

**Step-credit cluster now 16 entries**: prior 13 (ASCT, TEMPO, SIPO, DARS, T2SPO, TASPO, ProVer, FAULT, SHARPO, TRIAGE, AdaStep, DepGPO, GACA) + **BoT-GRPO** (GRPO integration), **Beyond Outcome Rewards** (retrieval domain), **RewardWeaver** (adaptive selection). BoT-GRPO is the first entry to solve the GRPO+process-reward integration problem without a value network. RewardWeaver introduces a meta-layer that selects *which* process reward to apply at each stage — orthogonal to all prior entries. Beyond Outcome Rewards extends the domain to retrieval/search agents.

**BoT-GRPO follow-up**: Does the bag-of-tokens aggregation work as well when the token-level reward model is itself trained with BoT-GRPO (bootstrapped)? The paper uses a pre-trained token-level RM; the closed-loop case is unexplored.

**RewardWeaver vs. GACA composability**: GACA selects *how much* episode vs. step credit to mix per decision; RewardWeaver selects *which* process rewards to apply per training stage. These are orthogonal — open question whether composing them (GACA inside a RewardWeaver stage) would yield further gains.

**Retrieval credit generalization**: Beyond Outcome Rewards studies retrieval step credit for search agents. Open question: does the framework generalize to other tool-call types (calculator, code execution, image retrieval), connecting it to the NTEP-R tool-call credit thread?
