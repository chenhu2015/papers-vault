**SPyCE closes the skill-reuse gap in multimodal agentic RL.** The vault's tool-use thread (SkillGate Aug 20, SSPO Aug 22) had established how to credit skill selection (SkillGate) and how to distill agentic steps with environmental evidence (SSPO). SPyCE now adds the missing piece: how to make skills reusable across tasks by co-evolving the skill library with the policy, avoiding both the "skills as frozen retrieval" failure (memory-based systems) and the "skills must be rediscovered from scratch" failure (scalar-reward RL). The workflow-skill level (high-level tool-use priors) is structurally equivalent to MileGPO's milestone templates but operates as a training prior rather than a credit signal.

**SPA-RL sits at the intersection of the credit assignment thread and MileGPO.** SPA's progress estimator is trained to decompose final outcome reward into stepwise contributions — but this is exactly the type of signal that "Credit Without Ground Truth" (Aug 21) critiques as tracking fluency rather than causal contribution. SPA predates "Credit Without Ground Truth" (published May 2025) and does not address this concern. The experiment that remains open: compare SPA's progress-based credit, MileGPO's PCC criterion, and counterfactual ground truth (Verifiable Counterfactual Supervision template injection) on the same ALFWorld trajectories. SPA at least reports +2.5% success rate over SOTA — which is a result "Credit Without Ground Truth" would call a "dose effect" unless controlled for sample size.

**GEAR and SSPO form a complementary reward-shaping pair.** SSPO (Aug 22) uses Evidence Anchors (web snippets confirming search findings) to differentiate steps during teacher-student distillation in the OPD setting. GEAR uses evidence-grounding rewards (overlap with annotated key evidence) in a standard RL training loop for long-context reasoning. Together they establish "evidence grounding" as a principle applicable across training paradigms (distillation vs. RL), task types (search agent vs. long-context QA), and reward levels (process-level advantage weighting vs. dense reward shaping).
