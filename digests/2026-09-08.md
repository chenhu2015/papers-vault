**TRIAL introduces hindsight conditioning as the fourth structural approach to per-turn credit redistribution.** The credit assignment thread through Sep 08: VICT (verifier-side atom extraction → proof-edge tracing), DiDPO (artifact-side code-diff groupability anchors), T-STAR (cross-trajectory cognitive tree → introspective valuation back-propagation), TRIAL (hindsight conditioning → signed log-probability gap → trajectory-relative normalization). All four methods are structurally orthogonal: VICT derives credit from verifier internal structure, DiDPO from output artifact structure, T-STAR from cross-sample trajectory structure, and TRIAL from the counterfactual distribution gap. This orthogonality suggests all four could be composed in a single training objective, and no such combination has been attempted.

**SAPO and SAO independently converge on "eliminate group sampling" as an efficiency axis.** SAPO replaces the group-wise value network with a shared autoregressive backbone at distinct causal boundaries (architectural convergence). SAO replaces group-wise rollout sampling with single-rollout-per-prompt (sampling convergence). Both achieve the same effect: removing GRPO's fixed-group requirement for advantage estimation. This convergence is significant because it suggests the group constraint in GRPO is the primary efficiency bottleneck in large-scale agentic RL — SAPO and SAO address it from different angles (value architecture vs. rollout sampling), and their combination has not been studied. SAO's production deployment in GLM-5.2 (750B-A40B) confirms viability at the largest scales.

**T-STAR's Cognitive Tree is the cross-trajectory analog of RTPO's reverse-tree.** RTPO recovers causal structure within a single trajectory (per-turn causal boundaries in multi-turn sequences). T-STAR recovers latent correlation structure across trajectory samples (by merging functionally similar steps from different rollouts into a unified tree). Both use tree back-propagation for advantage redistribution. The key distinction: RTPO's reverse-tree operates on the optimization dynamics within one rollout, while T-STAR's Cognitive Tree operates on the sample diversity across rollouts. Composing them — RTPO within-rollout reverse-tree + T-STAR cross-rollout cognitive tree — would address both intra-rollout and inter-rollout credit structure simultaneously; this combination is unexplored.

**The multi-turn reward taxonomy paper (today's 4th paper) provides the formal grounding for why the entire credit assignment thread converges on per-turn granularity.** The terminal/delayed/per-turn taxonomy establishes that the per-turn formulation is strictly richer than trajectory-level RL (terminal reward) or prefix-conditioned RL (delayed reward). Every credit assignment paper in the thread — VICT, DiDPO, TRIAL, T-STAR, SAPO, SAO, RTPO — is implicitly solving the same problem: finding the per-turn advantage signal that the per-turn MDP formulation requires but that sparse outcome rewards cannot provide. This unifying insight is absent from individual papers; the taxonomy makes it explicit.
