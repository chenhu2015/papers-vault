# SRPO: Self-Reflective Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon Reasoning

## Summary

SRPO enables LLMs to analyze their own completed trajectories, synthesize errors into concise "reflection patches," and use reflection-conditioned teacher scores on student rollouts as dense token-level training signals — converting sparse terminal rewards to dense supervision without external critics or larger teacher models. Qwen3-8B trained with SRPO reaches 73.3% on AIME'24 at only 8% of SFT training FLOPs, plus 76.8% on ALFWorld and 64.7% on WebShop. Self-reflection here acts as both credit assignment (localizing which steps were wrong) and signal generation (producing the corrective gradient).

## Key Contributions

- Self-reflection pipeline: model analyzes its own completed trajectories and synthesizes per-step "reflection patches" identifying errors
- Reflection-conditioned teacher scoring used as dense token-level training signal on student on-policy rollouts
- No external critic, separate reward model, or larger teacher model required
- Exceptional data efficiency: AIME'24 73.3% at 0.08x SFT FLOPs; WebShop 64.7%, ALFWorld 76.8%, SWE-Bench-Lite 31.2%

## Relevance

SRPO directly addresses the sparse-reward credit assignment problem for long-horizon agentic RL that has been a recurring thread in the vault (SPA-RL's progress estimator, IAPO's influence graph, HiDiffTIR's difficulty weighting). Unlike all prior vault approaches, SRPO's credit signal is generated by the model itself through self-reflection — removing the dependency on external annotators, verifiers, or value functions that DataPRM, GEAR, and SPA-RL all require.
