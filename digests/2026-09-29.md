## Open gaps (updated)

**Counterfactual credit cluster — now two strong entries**: ASCT (attentive tree, dense) and CRR (forkable environment, sparse). Both respond directly to the "Credit Without Ground Truth" confound by grounding advantage in executed outcomes rather than proxy signals. Open question: do ASCT and CRR methods generalise beyond their respective settings (RAG for ASCT, SWE sandboxes for CRR)? CRR explicitly notes that stochastic continuations and expensive/imperfect replay are limitations. A unified design that adapts branch density to environment cost remains an open goal.

**StraTA + PilotRL synthesis**: Both papers condition agentic RL on explicit high-level strategy/planning, but use different mechanisms — StraTA's diversity sampling in GRPO rollouts vs. PilotRL's staged curriculum. Open question: can PilotRL's progressive curriculum (imitation → plan RL → joint RL) be combined with StraTA's diverse strategy sampling to improve both sample efficiency and coverage?
