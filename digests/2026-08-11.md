**TCRM and SHAPE together formally close Gap #6 (TD credit + entropy gating synthesis).** Gap #6 has been open since July: no single paper combined TD-style temporal credit structures with entropy gating for LLM RL. TCRM closes the TD side: by regularizing reward models with MC+TD objectives, it makes token-level outputs equal to conditional expected rewards — every token position becomes a value estimate derived from temporal coherence rather than independent scoring. SHAPE closes the synthesis: it introduces a solvability-potential function as the segment-level credit source (analogous to a value function), and entropy-driven redistribution as the token-level allocation signal within each segment. The hierarchical SHAPE structure is the first explicit combination of both: potential estimation (giving temporal structure to segment-level credit) with entropy gating (sharpening allocation within segments). Gap #6 is **CLOSED** by TCRM (reward model → value function via TD) + SHAPE (hierarchical potential + entropy synthesis).

**MBPO introduces a sixth structural class in the multimodal VLM RL credit taxonomy.** The vault's existing VLM RL credit mechanisms: IAPO (visual faithfulness as reward), ViGOS (decoupled perception-reasoning OPD with separate teachers), VAD (counterfactual visual evidence attribution in OPD corrections), OPPO (cue-decomposed reward + masked-input KL for omni-modal faithfulness), CFPO (counterfactual visual-cue suppression regularization in RL). MBPO adds a fundamentally different mechanism: **tree-structured exploration at VL decision boundaries with branch-relative credit**. Where the existing mechanisms all operate on the reward/supervision signal (what signal to use and how to decompose it), MBPO operates on the rollout structure (where to branch the trajectory). The branch-relative advantage is a natural counterfactual baseline that doesn't require any separate teacher, reward model, or attribution method — the counterfactual is provided by the sibling branch under the same visual context.

**CFPO bridges the CSCR counterfactual thread with the multimodal RL training thread.** CSCR (Aug 9) applied counterfactual outcome conditioning to diagnose token-level credit unreliability. CFPO applies counterfactual visual-cue suppression as a training-time regularization signal. The key difference from CSCR: CSCR asks "does the teacher direction change when the outcome label is flipped?" (a diagnostic for credit reliability); CFPO asks "does the policy prediction change when visual cues are removed?" (a training signal for visual grounding). The connection to OPPO's masked-input KL is direct: OPPO masks modality-specific evidence tokens to enforce cross-modal faithfulness; CFPO suppresses critical visual cues to enforce causal grounding. Both apply KL-style divergence under input masking, but OPPO targets the reward-level signal and CFPO targets the policy-gradient-level regularization.

**Visual Shortcut Dynamics provides timing guidance for all visual faithfulness methods.** The vault's entire visual faithfulness thread (IAPO, ViGOS, VAD, OPPO, CFPO) assumes that applying faithfulness regularization helps. This paper asks *when*. The sharp onset + hysteresis finding means: (1) faithfulness regularization applied before the shortcut onset is highly efficient and prevents consolidation; (2) the same regularization applied after consolidation faces an asymmetric recovery problem. This has direct implications for CFPO (which applies counterfactual regularization throughout training — confirmed effective by the pre-onset window finding) and for ViGOS (which applies OPD-level faithfulness supervision from the start — also consistent with early-application benefit). The critical intervention window finding is a new Gap in the vault: **Gap #24: How to detect shortcut onset in real-time during training**, to trigger faithfulness regularization at the right moment.

**Updated step-level credit taxonomy (extended):** Token-level (ADRS-TVA, PCSD, OCSD, DASH, CSCR, RP-OPSD), step-level (Guided-OPD, FutureBridge-OPD, Gated-BEPO), trajectory-level (TACO/DAPR counterfactual, IBPO implicit multi-trajectory), path-level (Parallel Shapley), **segment-level** (SHAPE hierarchical potential+entropy, MBPO branch-relative VL). SHAPE and MBPO introduce a distinct granularity between step-level and trajectory-level: segments span multiple tokens but are shorter than full trajectories. SHAPE defines segments by solvability stages; MBPO defines segments by branch boundaries at VL decision points.
