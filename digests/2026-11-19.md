## Open gaps (updated)

**Multi-turn OPSD cluster (now 7 entries)**: +SERO (executable oracle continuations; policy-reachability weighting) + RIPPLE (effect-aware credit; Critique-to-Action Handoff Gap). Now: SAGE-OPD (turn-level selective intervention), Skill-SD (trajectory→skill PI), ActFirst-OPD (act-first/reason-later decoupling), MVPO (milestone viability potential), EMPO (effect-marginal policy optimization), SERO (executable oracle continuation), RIPPLE (stage-decomposed recovery with process-level teacher). Seven orthogonal mechanisms spanning: when/how to apply guidance, what to use as PI, how to decouple interaction/reasoning, how to handle zero-credit failures, how to align credit with execution equivalence, how to extend supervision to future oracle trajectories, and how to decompose recovery into stages.

**Agentic orchestration RL (new, 1 entry)**: E³-Orch adds a multi-agent orchestration angle to the agentic RL cluster. This is distinct from single-policy agentic RL — the orchestrator trains on milestone/plan decisions across a team of specialized executors.

**GRPO objective variants cluster (now 4+ entries)**: +APMPO (power-mean generalization of GRPO objective). Now includes MinPRO (prefix IS ratio), LGPO (on-policy ratio), EPIC (exact prefix IS), APMPO (power-mean aggregation). APMPO is the first entry addressing the aggregation function rather than the importance ratio.

**Offline distillation with negative traces (new, 1 entry)**: REDI introduces REINFORCE-style offline distillation using rejected teacher traces. Orthogonal to OPSD (online) and to DPO (preference pairs) — it's offline REINFORCE with teacher-generated positives and negatives.
