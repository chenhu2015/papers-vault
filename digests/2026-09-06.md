**GAR adds a seventh axis to the GRPO improvement design map.** The six axes established through Sep 05: reward signal (StructReward dense process), advantage estimator (MaxPO L2O centering + OTB per-token reweighting), rollout tree efficiency (HARTS prefix sharing), environment model (WM-R1 world-model-embedded thinking), rollout diversity (NC-GRPO latent Gaussian noise), grounding density (VIG information-theoretic token reward). GAR adds a seventh: gradient-space reward alignment — using cosine similarity between the rollout gradient and an expert-anchor gradient as a dense reward signal. GAR is orthogonal to all prior axes: it operates on the reward signal side (like StructReward and VIG) but derives signal from the gradient space rather than the output or representation space. The multiplicative decomposition (prediction-error × activation-pattern) provides theoretical grounding absent from StructReward and VIG, which are justified empirically.

**DARS confirms StructReward + OTB composability in multimodal RL, partially closing an open gap.** The "StructReward + OTB combination" gap — no paper combining dense process reward with token-level advantage reweighting — was listed as open since Sep 03. DARS implements both: prefix-gated reward (structured reasoning field boundaries = StructReward principle) and token-level advantage reweighting (planner-level credit attribution = OTB principle), in the image-editing domain. The cross-module credit assignment (planner vs. renderer routing) is a new dimension not covered by either prior paper. This confirms composability but in a heterogeneous pipeline (LLM + diffusion model), not a single-model GRPO setting — the gap for single-model StructReward + OTB in math/general reasoning remains.

**Evidence-RL merges two threads (VIG + CSR) into a single VLM training objective.** VIG (Sep 04) scored each reasoning token by mutual information with the image; CSR (Sep 03) penalized models when logically invalid traces still produce correct outputs. CED combines the structural insight of both: it uses counterfactual region neutralization (CSR's causal perturbation operator) applied to spatial evidence regions (VIG's visual information content target). This convergence suggests a unified principle — "counterfactual causal necessity" — that operates across both linguistic reasoning (CSR) and visual grounding (CED) faithfulness failure modes. Extending this principle to the other three failure modes in the five-mode stack (CTA skill attribution, FACE-Eval CoT-tool return, REDAgentBench verbalization-execution) is the natural next research step.

**TPAW advances the Agon generalization thread to general alignment.** Agon (Sep 04) was demonstrated only on math (DeepMath). The self-play vocabulary search (today's Search 1) mapped to a specific architecture: current policy vs. historical checkpoint pool. TPAW operationalizes this architecture with dual adaptive weighting and demonstrates it on general alignment benchmarks. The structural difference from Agon (historical vs. live rival) has an important implication: TPAW's historical-checkpoint pool provides curriculum stability (champions from past training are known-good), while Agon's live rival provides dynamic pressure (rival is also improving). The two mechanisms are complementary and their combination has not been studied.

**RFEval (excluded from results but surfaced in search 4) confirms the urgency of the five-failure-mode unified training problem.** RFEval finds that RL post-training reduces reasoning faithfulness in 49.7% of outputs even when accuracy is maintained — and that adding RL-style objectives on top of SFT can *degrade* faithfulness. This directly validates the five-failure-mode diagnosis (CTA/FACE-Eval/REDAgentBench/CSR/Influence-Is-Not-Authority) and implies that current GRPO training (optimized purely for accuracy/verifiable outcomes) may be systematically worsening faithfulness across all five failure modes simultaneously. No unified training fix has been found across 6+ weeks of searches.

**LUT (5th paper) closes the NC-GRPO + CSR convergence gap in latent reasoning space.** NC-GRPO (Sep 03) diversified rollouts by injecting Gaussian noise into the hidden layer. CSR (Sep 03) penalized causally unnecessary reasoning steps. LUT's Latent Attribution Policy Optimization differentially weights latent reasoning steps by their answer-attribution score — operationalizing CSR's causal necessity criterion in NC-GRPO's latent space. The information-gain trajectory selection (SFT stage) also parallels GAR's gradient-space alignment: both use internal model signals to identify high-value training examples without external annotation, suggesting a broader principle of "internal signal-guided training" orthogonal to reward model or verifiable-outcome approaches.
