**PAIR + CSO + RSPO together define a three-layer approach to multi-turn agentic process reward design.** The vault's existing agentic RL process supervision mechanisms (SOD divergence-adaptive gating, VPS adaptive weighting, VERDICT disagreement consensus) all operate at inference time or with external verifiers. PAIR, CSO, and RSPO address the training pipeline itself: PAIR provides the internal reward source (LLM hidden states replace external RM); CSO provides the selection criterion (only outcome-flipping critical steps receive DPO supervision); RSPO provides the training objective (process rewards diversify sampling, outcome rewards define the gradient). The three layers are complementary — process reward source × critical step selection × objective alignment — and represent a complete pipeline for dense-signal multi-turn agent RL without external dependencies.

**OGLS-SD completes a 2×2 taxonomy of external-teacher-free supervision.** Prior entries: SSOPD (outcome-conditioned × distillation-signal level — correct/wrong group KL) and NOPD (input-perturbation × distillation-signal level — clean/noisy input KL). OGLS-SD adds outcome-conditioned × teacher-logit level — contrasting successful/failed teacher logits to construct a steering direction applied to the teacher before KL. The fourth cell (input-perturbation × teacher-logit level) remains unexplored: no paper yet steers the teacher logit using input-corruption contrast rather than outcome contrast. This is the new synthesis target replacing the less specific "NOPD+SSOPD combination" framing from Aug 14.

**SRGPO's step grouping closes the within-trajectory granularity gap in the group-relative advantage hierarchy.** The vault has tracked: rollout-group GRPO (standard), stage-group Temporal GRPO (Aug 14, same-stage rollout comparison). SRGPO introduces step-group (grouping steps within a single trajectory for step-level relative advantage). Together these define a three-level "group relative" hierarchy: step-group (SRGPO, finest) → stage-group (Temporal GRPO) → rollout-group (GRPO, coarsest). Each level trades off credit granularity vs. variance of the advantage estimate.

**CSO's policy-reachability filter is a new constraint in the verified process supervision thread.** VPS (verifiable intermediate claims), VPRM (rule-based step verifiers), VERDICT (disagreement consensus), and SeeNav-Agent/SRGPO (task-structure step rewards) all define what makes a step signal verifiable. CSO adds a fourth dimension: *reachability* — a verified alternative is only used for training if the policy itself can successfully execute from that alternative to task completion. This prevents training on alternatives that are expert-reachable but policy-unreachable, directly addressing the off-policy distribution shift in DPO that CSO identifies as the core failure mode of naive step-level preference learning.
