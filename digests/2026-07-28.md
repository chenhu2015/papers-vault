HyGAE (0.91) is the most structurally important paper of the day. It provides the missing theoretical bridge in the credit assignment thread: Progress Advantage (Jul 26) proved the optimal advantage exists for general MDPs; TRACE (Jul 25) implemented it at the turn level; PRPO (Jul 26) implemented it at the visual-token level; HyGAE proves that both token-level and turn-level objectives can be jointly optimized by a single unified critic — no separate networks, no hierarchy of critics. The exact analytic form finding (ablation showing approximate hybrid advantage fails) is important: it means the theoretical derivation matters in practice, not just in principle. This completes the theoretical picture of multi-granularity credit assignment for VLM RL that has been the central thread since OPPO (Jul 24, Bayesian token↔trajectory recursion) and TRACE (Jul 25, turn-level TD log-ratio).

O²-CritiCuRL (0.82) fills a specific gap in the step importance estimation thread. STAPO (Jul 26) uses normalized entropy (deviation from agent's own mean) to flag neglect-prone steps; O²-CritiCuRL uses multi-rollout frequency analysis on step-annotated trajectories to distinguish critical from redundant steps. These are complementary signals: STAPO's normalized entropy is online and does not require step annotations; O²-CritiCuRL's offline analysis requires step labels but provides a more interpretable importance estimate (steps that appear consistently across many rollouts as decision-critical). The offline-online paradigm also addresses the static supervision limitation: rather than fixing which steps are important at the start, O²-CritiCuRL iterates offline re-labeling against the evolving policy.

EmoAgent-R1 (0.80) introduces P-GRPO's PMI-based modulation as the first information-theoretic approach to GRPO token weighting. The PMI signal (how much does this token's identity co-vary with the correctness of the outcome?) is distinct from: STAPO's normalized entropy (how uncertain is the model here?), PRPO's RVD (how visually grounded is this token?), GRAIL's gradient saliency (how large is this token's gradient?), and OPPO's Bayesian recursion (what is the posterior probability of this token given eventual success?). The PMI framing connects GRPO credit assignment to information theory, potentially offering a principled connection to mutual information-based reward shaping (a thread not yet explored in the vault).

MemAgent (0.79) and ARMOR (0.75) are solid contributions to existing threads. MemAgent provides a different architectural answer to CompactionRL's question (overwrite-based memory agent vs. joint task+compaction optimization). ARMOR provides a sample-diversity stabilization mechanism orthogonal to the reward-signal-design approaches (variance-profile, group filtering) that Gap #16 is tracking.

**New finding — HyGAE as synthesis of credit assignment thread:** HyGAE's unified critic proof retroactively explains why TRACE's turn-level critic + PRPO's token-level reshaping are compatible and can be unified: they are both approximating different components of the hybrid advantage under the same MDP. The unified critic result means any lab that has trained a value network for turn-level credit (as in TRACE) can re-use it for token-level credit estimation without modification.
