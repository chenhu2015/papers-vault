**"Credit Without Ground Truth" is the most disruptive paper of the digest and challenges foundational assumptions in the entire vault.** The vault's agentic RL training papers (SkillGate, RTPO, MileGPO, GUPO, PlanPO) all rely on step-level credit signals — whether milestone inference, selector credit, reverse-tree assignment, or gradient uncertainty weighting. This paper shows that in ALFWorld (a standard single-agent tool environment), none of these signal families identifies *causally* important steps better than chance. The key distinction: step correctness (which existing papers optimize) vs. step contribution (causal counterfactual impact). Fluency tracks correctness; neither tracks contribution. The seven-arm training experiment is decisive: no credit rule reliably outperforms the untrained policy. Apparent gains are dose effects (sample size variation), not credit content. **This does not invalidate the vault's papers** — they may be effective for other reasons (exploration shaping, regularization, curriculum effects) — but it does call for caution in interpreting step-level credit results.

**PlanPO completes the three-axis GRPO failure diagnosis for multi-turn agentic settings.** The vault now has three orthogonal fixes for GRPO in multi-turn agents: (1) GUPO (Aug 19) — gradient aggregation conflicts across queries (Bayesian-Dirichlet uncertainty weighting); (2) RTPO (Aug 20) — temporal credit instability across turns (reverse-tree formulation); (3) PlanPO (today) — advantage collapse within the success group (coarse-to-fine length-conditioned advantages). Each addresses a different failure mode of the same base algorithm. Together they form a comprehensive GRPO enhancement stack for agentic training.

**MileGPO and "Credit Without Ground Truth" must be read together.** MileGPO introduces Milestone Discovery and Progress-Contrastive Calibration as improved credit signals — but "Credit Without Ground Truth" shows that ALFWorld (MileGPO's primary evaluation environment) is exactly the setting where standard step-level signals fail to correlate with causal contribution. MileGPO's PCC mechanism (comparing against same-state alternatives) is structurally closest to the counterfactual ground truth methodology — but uses rollout groups rather than executed replay. Whether PCC's local-progress criterion correlates with causal contribution is an open empirical question.

**AlignDistil + GC-OPD + R2-OPD form a three-paper OPD correction cluster addressing complementary failure modes.** AlignDistil (RLHF↔distillation theoretical bridge) shows when and why the two approaches are equivalent. GC-OPD addresses the long-context failure mode (teacher-verifier disagreement, local vs. global coherence). R2-OPD (outside cap, 0.80) addresses the reasoning-progress conflict (teacher rewards conflict with genuine reasoning advancement). Together they provide a theory + two-axis empirical fix for OPD's limitations.
